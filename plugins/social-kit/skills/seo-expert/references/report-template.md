# Report Template

Use this structure. Adapt section depth to what discovery found; drop a section rather than pad it.

```
# [Brand] SEO Strategy for Organic Growth
[Date] · Prepared for [user or agency]

## Summary
Three to five sentences: where the brand stands, the biggest constraint, and the three moves that matter most. State any assumptions made because information was missing.

## What we found
### The brand today
Business model, market, goal, how it converts. Site status (live, pre-launch, rebuild).

### Winner traits scorecard
| Trait | Status | Evidence | Gap |
(offers product/service, task completion, proprietary assets, tight focus, strong brand)

### Technical health
Blockers first (server rendering, indexation, AI crawler access), then secondary issues. Cite what was observed (script output, fetched pages).

### Search landscape
Who ranks for the brand's most valuable queries and what that means. Brand footprint off-site: reviews, lists, press, video, communities.

### AI visibility
What can be observed, plus the prompt set for the user to run and how to record results.

## Strategy
### Phase 1: Foundations (weeks 1 to 4)
### Phase 2: Positioning (months 1 to 3)
### Phase 3: Authority and AI visibility (ongoing)
### Phase 4: Measurement and testing (ongoing)

For each recommendation:
- **What:** specific action for this brand
- **Why:** the finding behind it and its source
- **Confidence:** strong / moderate / weak
- **Effort:** low / medium / high

## What we're not recommending
Tactics the evidence shows waste effort that the brand might otherwise consider, each with a one-line reason.

## KPIs
Business outcome metric, branded demand, visibility split (branded vs non-branded), AI share of voice by topic. Include a baseline to capture now.

## Caveats
Evidence limits that bear on this plan (correlational data, vendor sources, recent updates). Date of the evidence base.

## Sources
Linked list of the independent sources cited.
```

## Style
- Plain, direct sentences. No em dashes; restructure the sentence instead.
- Specific to the brand: name its pages, competitors, queries and assets.
- Tables for scorecards and roadmaps, prose for reasoning.
