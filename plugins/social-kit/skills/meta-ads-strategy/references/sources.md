## Known limits

- Large datasets (Motion, Haus, CTC) skew to DTC ecommerce with heavy spend.
- Haus and Motion sell products related to their findings.
- Loomer's Master Brief is a July 2026 snapshot that he expects to evolve.
- Sri Lanka cost figures conflict and cover domestic audiences only.

## Refresh procedure

Run monthly, or whenever a major Meta platform change is reported.

1. Rerun the research with the same source rule: independent practitioners and third-party data only, no Meta-published material.
2. Prioritize changes to: attribution and reporting, Advantage+ behaviour, creative benchmarks, Special Ad Category rules, learning-phase thresholds and new incrementality data.
3. Update the affected figures in `playbook.md` and `verticals.md`, keeping each claim attributed.
4. Update the disagreement table in `playbook.md` section 8 if any expert has changed position.
5. Bump the refresh date at the top of this file.
6. Do not change `SKILL.md` unless the workflow itself needs to change.
