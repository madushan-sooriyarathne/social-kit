## 1. How to read the numbers

- Socialinsider and Quid (Rival IQ) divide engagement by followers. Buffer divides by reach. Dash Social uses views on very large brands (average 2.3M followers). That is why "carousels beat Reels" and "Reels beat carousels" can both be true. Always state the denominator when quoting a figure.
- Buffer, Metricool and Emplifi analyse their own customers' accounts, so effects are correlational. Buffer and Virev use within-account comparisons, which controls for account-level differences.
- Hootsuite's experiments are small (around 10 posts) and directional only.

## 2. Platform-wide trends

- Instagram average reach rate is 4.90% of followers, down 14% year over year (Socialinsider, 872,075 brand posts, Jan 2025 to Aug 2026). Facebook Page reach averages 1.80%.
- Metricool 2026 Social Media Study (39.7M posts, 1.06M accounts): Instagram post reach down 31%, Reels reach down 35% year over year.
- Instagram engagement by followers: 0.48% in 2025, 0.45% by Q2-Q3 2026 (Socialinsider). Quid median 0.30%, down from 0.36% (150 companies in each of 18 industries).
- Small accounts reach a larger share of followers: median nano creator Reel views equal 64% of followers vs 14% above 1M (Virev, 10.4M Reels).
- Instagram views from non-followers rose from 30% to 49% (Dash Social 2026 benchmarks). Discovery now runs mostly through non-followers.

## 3. Instagram formats

Reach rate by account size (Socialinsider, 140K Reels, H1 2026):

| Followers | Reels | Carousels | Images |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-5K | 9.78% | 8.80% | 7.00% |
| 5-10K | 7.55% | 7.40% | 6.35% |
| 10-50K | 7.10% | 6.40% | 5.50% |
| 50-100K | 5.60% | 5.85% | 4.70% |
| 100K-1M | 5.00% | 6.00% | 5.00% |

Takeaway: Reels win reach below 50K followers; carousels match or beat them above 50K.

- Absolute reach (Metricool, 24.3M posts): Reels 14,918 accounts, carousels 11,573, single images 4,779. Reels reach 29% more people than carousels. Carousels get the most views (27,797 vs 23,074) because people swipe back.
- Engagement rate: Socialinsider (by followers, 35M posts) carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%. Buffer (by reach) carousels 6.90%, Reels 3.31%. Dash Social (by views, very large brands) Reels 2.7%, carousels 1.4%.
- Carousels get 9x the saves of single images, yet brands use them 37% less (Metricool).
- Single images: reach down 21.96%, engagement down 45.98% year over year (Metricool).

## 4. Instagram Reels specifics

- Length (Socialinsider reach rate): under 30s 5.20%, 30-60s 5.60%, 60-90s 5.30%, 90-120s 4.30%, over 2 min 3.50%. Virev: below 3 minutes length moves views by 4% or less; over 3 minutes gets 0.76x views. Rule: 30-60s for reach, under 90s generally, never over 3 minutes without a reason.
- Skip rate (leave within 3 seconds): 65.5% at 1-5K accounts, 60.5% at 100K-1M (Socialinsider). The first frame decides performance.
- Average Reel watch time is 8.5 seconds, more than double the prior year (Metricool).
- Captions, on-screen text and spoken audio feed Instagram search (Metricool 2026 Reels guide). Say the topic aloud and show it on screen.
- Shares: Reels average 711 shares per post, 27% above the Instagram average (Dash Social). Reels generate the most reposts at every account size (Socialinsider).
- Typical brand cadence: 8 Reels a month at the smallest sizes, up to 20 at the largest (Socialinsider).

## 5. Instagram Stories

- Reach is mostly followers: 9.55-10.40% of followers at 1-5K accounts, falling to 0.50-0.65% at 100K-1M (Socialinsider, Jan to May 2025). Video Stories out-reach image Stories at every size.
- Exit rates: 23.8% on frame 1, 20.5% on frame 2, 18.5% on frame 3, flattening near 12.5% by frame 15. Reach rate climbs from 6.3% with one Story to about 20.5% by the sixth (Socialinsider). Open with the strongest frame; run 4-8 frame sequences.
- Brands average 2 Stories a day, about 70% completion, about 5% exit rate (Dash Social). Volume scales with size from 12 a month (1-5K) to 80 a month (100K-1M) (Socialinsider).
- Story replies rose 88% year over year (Metricool). Use Stories for conversation and retention; do not judge them on reach.
- Some schedulers strip interactive stickers on auto-publish; post interactive Stories natively.

## 6. Posting frequency

- Buffer (2.1M Instagram posts, 102K accounts): weekly follower growth 0.12% at 1-2 posts/week, 0.26% at 3-5, 0.44% at 6-9. Reach per post about 12% higher at 3-5/week and about 24% higher at 10+.
- Buffer 2026 (4.8M channel-weeks): a "no-post penalty" where silent weeks underperform the account's own baseline. Same report finds reach per post tends to decline as frequency rises, which conflicts with the 2025 study.
- Virev within-creator model: frequency moves Reel views by 9% or less.
- Interpretation: consistency drives total growth; extra volume does not reliably raise reach per post.

## 7. Captions, keywords and hashtags

- Posts with at least one hashtag got 31.70% fewer views than average (Metricool, correlational). 3-5 hashtags gave 1.04x views (Virev), effectively no effect.
- No caption: 0.82x views vs a 21-100 character caption (Virev).
- Keyword captions got about 30% more reach than hashtag captions (Hootsuite, 10 posts, 2022; directional only).
- Questions in posts: +36.70% comments. Comment-focused CTA: +202.78% comments (Metricool).

## 8. Collab posts

- Emplifi (520,198 posts, 3,791 brand profiles, H1 2026): over 73% of brands use collabs. Creator-published collabs accepted by the brand perform best, with more than 5x amplification in ecommerce and retail and about 5x interactions for the smallest brands.
- Collab posts get 1.52x the views of a creator's own Reels, mostly benefiting small creators (Virev).
- Collab reach behaves like a weighted average of partners' reach, not a sum (peer-reviewed, 100 collab posts, Journal of Digital and Social Media Marketing, 2026). Partner with accounts of equal or larger reach.
- Have the creator publish and invite the brand as collaborator.

## 9. Community management

Buffer (Julian Winternheimer, nearly 2M posts across six platforms), correlational lift from replying to comments:
- Threads +42% (128,000 posts, about two-thirds of accounts improved)
- LinkedIn +30%
- Instagram +21% (700,000+ posts, 68,000 accounts, 63% improved)
- Facebook +9.5% (1M+ posts, 54% of Pages improved)
- X +8%, Bluesky +5%

Most views arrive within the first three days (Metricool), so reply actively in that window.

## 10. Originality

- From April 30, 2026, Instagram accounts that mostly repost others' photos and carousels became ineligible for recommendations (reported by TechCrunch, eMarketer, Tubefilter). Reels already had this rule.
- Viral claims of 60-80% reach drops for aggregators are unverified.
- Practical rules: no watermarked cross-platform uploads; recut and re-caption cross-posted video; get rights to UGC and transform it before posting.
- No penalty found for third-party scheduling or CapCut-edited Reels (Hootsuite small tests).

## 11. Timing

- Sprout Social (about 2B engagements, 307K profiles, Nov 2025 to Feb 2026): Instagram best Mon 2-4pm, Tue 1-7pm, Wed 12-9pm, Thu 12-2pm. Facebook best Tue and Wed 12-8pm. Weekends weakest.
- Buffer (9.6M Instagram posts): Thu 9am, Wed 12pm, Wed 6pm. Fri and Sat weakest.
- Posting hour moves views by only 7% (Virev).
- Rule: midweek, midday to early evening in the audience's time zone, then stop optimising timing.

## 12. Facebook

Reach by format (Socialinsider, 2025 to Aug 2026): images 1.20%, albums 1.15%, status posts 0.80%, Reels 0.55%, links 0.20%.

- Engagement (Socialinsider, 25M posts): average 0.15%, flat. Best format by Page size: albums below 5K, status posts 5-50K, Reels above 50K. Albums get the most shares. Reels get roughly 4-6x the views of other formats at every size.
- Buffer 2025 medians (by reach): images 5.20%, video 4.84%, text 4.76%, links 4.43%; Buffer concludes format gaps are small.
- Reel length: engagement peaks at 120-180 seconds (Socialinsider, 5.8M Facebook Reels, 0.25%, also most shares; 2026 video study shows the same 2-3 minute peak at 0.50%).
- Typical Page posts 34 times a month, 3.8% median engagement, 0.5% monthly growth (Buffer, 52M posts, 213K accounts). Brands cut posting 22% with flat engagement (Socialinsider), so volume is not the lever.
- Links: put URLs in a comment or use an image post. This is practitioner advice, not verified with data; frame it as a test.

## 13. Threads

- Median engagement 3.6% in 2025, down from 4.4% (Buffer). The widely quoted 6.25% figure is outdated 2024 data.
- By format: video 5.55%, images 4.55%, text 2.79%, links 2.34% (Buffer, 52M+ posts).
- Average 1,536 impressions per post at 6.4 posts a week; 100K-1M accounts post 53+ times a week; only 17% of accounts grew (Metricool).
- Buffer advises 1-3+ posts a day including replies (guidance, not a measured result). Post conversationally; add video when possible.

## 14. Industry notes

- Reels clearly win in hospitality and hotels (0.38% engagement vs 0.23% for carousels), restaurants and travel (Socialinsider).
- Reels have no engagement edge in e-commerce and jewelry; carousels and product-led content hold up (Socialinsider).
- Creator-published collabs are strongest in ecommerce and retail (Emplifi).

## 15. KPIs and benchmarks

| KPI | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Instagram reach rate (reach / followers) | 4.90% average; see tier table in section 3 |
| Facebook reach rate | 1.80% average |
| Instagram engagement by followers | 0.45-0.48% (Socialinsider); 0.30% median (Quid) |
| Reels skip rate | 60-65%; aim below |
| Reel watch time | 8.5s average |
| Story frame 1 exit rate | 23.8% |
| Weekly follower growth at 3-5 posts/week | about 0.26% |
| Facebook monthly growth | about 0.5% |
| Threads engagement | 3.6% median |

Also track shares and saves per reach, share of views from non-followers, and each post against the account's own median.

## 16. What to avoid

- Single-image-heavy feeds
- Link posts on Facebook (0.20% reach)
- Reels over 3 minutes on Instagram
- Posting without a caption
- Hashtag stuffing as a growth strategy
- Reposting others' content or watermarked cross-platform video
- Going silent for weeks
- Ignoring comments
- Judging Stories by reach
- Benchmarking against accounts of a different size

## 17. Thin-evidence areas

No large independent studies found for Lives, broadcast channels, Facebook Groups (the "Groups get far more reach" claim traces to vendor blogs without data) or Story sticker effects. Present these as tests.
