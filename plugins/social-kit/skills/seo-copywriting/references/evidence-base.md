# Evidence Base for SEO Copywriting (independent sources only, current to November 2026)

Strength labels: **Strong** = controlled single-variable tests; **Moderate** = large correlational datasets or consistent findings across independent analysts; **Weak** = small samples, self-reported case studies, dated or vendor marketing data.

## 1. Keyword placement

- **Kyle Roof (PageOptimizer Pro):** 400+ single-variable ranking tests; holds US Patent 10,540,263 B1 for the testing method. The four zones that matter most are the URL, title tag, H1 and a body paragraph mention. He describes getting those right as about 60% of SEO. Roof also ranked a lorem ipsum page for "rhinoplasty Plano" by tuning term placement alone. **Strong** for isolating on-page signals, but tests use controlled pages and invented keywords, so they understate links and brand in competitive SERPs.
- **Backlinko (11.8M results):** top-10 pages contain 65% to 85% of their ranking keyword in the title, yet a keyword-optimized title has near-zero correlation with position within page one. Backlinko calls these "ticket to entry" factors. **Moderate**, data from about 2019 to 2020.
- **Backlinko on-page guide (Brian Dean):** front-load the keyword in the title; use it once in the first 100 to 150 words; one H1 containing the keyword; short readable slug. **Moderate** (practitioner guidance).
- **Surfer (1M SERPs, 2025):** keywords in the URL path showed almost no correlation. This conflicts with Roof; include the keyword in the slug anyway because it costs nothing and helps readability. **Moderate, vendor.**

## 2. Coverage, originality and repetition

- **Surfer 2025:** pages using keyword variations performed best; exact-match use showed almost no correlation; keyword density described as irrelevant; topical coverage (entities, facts, subtopics) the strongest on-page factor. Surfer's own Content Score correlated 0.28 with rank (vendor measuring its own metric, no independent replication). **Moderate, vendor.**
- **Surfer:** pages repeating the same keyword in every heading underperformed pages using variations. **Moderate, vendor.**
- **Backlinko:** clear correlation between Clearscope Content Grade (comprehensiveness) and rankings, on a subset of about 1,000 keywords. **Moderate.**
- **Rankability (1,536 results, 32 keywords):** top-10 pages averaged about 0.04% exact-match density with no consistent correlation. **Weak (small sample).**
- **Lily Ray (Amsive):** performance mostly comes down to how much original insight and net-new information a page has; use expert quotes, original data, original imagery. **Moderate (consistent with core update analyses).**
- **Ahrefs (Louise Linehan case study):** adding tangential sections to a declining article diluted intent and cut traffic further; reverting helped. **Weak (single case), but directionally consistent.**
- **Lily Ray, May 2026:** patterns that "work until they don't": mass "X vs Y" pages, programmatic "what is X" glossaries, self-promotional listicles ranking the publisher first, one-question-per-URL FAQ farms, off-topic content at scale. **Moderate.**

## 3. Length

- Backlinko: average first-page result about 1,447 words but no direct relationship between word count and rank. **Moderate.**
- Ahrefs (about 900M pages): backlinks correlate positively with length up to about 1,000 words and negatively beyond; organic traffic shows a moderate negative correlation beyond 2,000 words. **Moderate.**
- Ahrefs (174,048 pages cited across 560,346 AI Overviews): 0.04 Spearman correlation between word count and citation position; 53.4% of citations went to pages under 1,000 words. **Moderate.**
- Rule: match the length range of the pages that rank for the intent. Never pad.

## 4. Titles and meta descriptions

- **Zyppy (Cyrus Shepard, 80,959 titles, 2,370 sites):** titles rewritten 61% of the time, mostly when too long or too short. Matching title and H1 reduced rewrites. Brackets removed more often (32%) than parentheses (19.7%). **Moderate.**
- **Ahrefs (20,000 keywords, 192,656 pages, 2020):** meta descriptions rewritten 62.78% of the time (65.62% on long-tail); 25.02% of top pages had none. **Moderate, dated.** Write meta descriptions for clicks, not rankings.

## 5. Search volume and difficulty data

- **Ahrefs (72,635 keywords vs Search Console impressions):** Keyword Planner overestimated volume 91.45% of the time. **Moderate.**
- **Ahrefs, Nov 2021:** "roughly accurate" defined as within 50% of Search Console impressions; Ahrefs' own estimates roughly accurate 60% of the time vs 45.22% for Keyword Planner, which drastically overestimates 54.28% of the time. **Moderate, vendor.**
- **Difficulty scores:** Ahrefs KD uses only referring domains to the top 10. Semrush KD% blends median referring domains, dofollow ratio, Authority Score of ranking domains and SERP features (0-14 very easy, 15-29 achievable for new domains focused on intent, 85-100 very hard). Neither measures content quality or intent fit.
- **Ahrefs practice:** filter KD no higher than your Domain Rating; prefer Traffic Potential (traffic of the #1 page across all its keywords) over single-keyword volume; use "Lowest DR" to find terms where a site like yours already ranks.
- Use volume to rank options against each other, never to forecast traffic.

## 6. Search intent and page type

- **Ahrefs:** four intents (informational, navigational, commercial investigation, transactional), plus local and branded flags. Read the "three Cs" from the live SERP: content type, format, angle. If the SERP is ecommerce pages and you sell nothing, ranking is very hard. For mixed SERPs pick the dominant intent or build separate pages. **Moderate.**
- **Kyle Roof:** search the term first; the ranking pages show what page type the engine expects. **Strong (practitioner tests).**
- **Backlinko (Amy Copadis, 2026):** for commercial pages, keyword research means confirming the buyer is ready to act. Light sales pages underperform in AI-shaped results, but avoid turning commercial pages into recipe-blog style essays. **Weak to moderate.**

## 7. Featured snippets and AI answers

- Snippet paragraph answers cluster around 40 to 50 words (Ghergich & Co./Semrush); Portent found very few paragraph snippets over 320 characters. **Moderate, dated (2017 to 2020).**
- Ahrefs (2M snippets): 99.58% come from top-10 pages. You must rank first. **Moderate.**
- Ahrefs AI Overviews: appear on 21% of keywords, 57.9% of question queries, 46.4% of 7+ word queries; 99.9% of AI Overview keywords informational. Ranking for an AI Overview's fan-out sub-queries correlated 0.77 with being cited. **Moderate, vendor.**
- Ahrefs CTR: AI Overviews cut position-1 CTR 34.5% (April 2025 study) and 58% (December 2025 rerun). Informational keywords now trade clicks for visibility; weigh them lower for conversion goals. **Moderate.**
- Ahrefs Louise Linehan: burying the lead delays an LLM's understanding of the page; answer early. **Weak to moderate.**
- Kevin Indig (70-user AI Overview study): brand recognition and trust act as the first filter; users skim AI answers and verify elsewhere, especially on YMYL. **Moderate (qualitative).**

## 8. Topical authority and clustering

- **Ahrefs Parent Topic:** keywords the same top page ranks for belong on one page; if the parent topic has more traffic potential, target it. **Moderate.**
- **Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR:** topical maps and a connected page network covering a topic fully; self-reported gains (1,100% in six months on one site; 200 to 154,000+ daily clicks for a SaaS brand). **Weak (self-reported case studies).**
- Hub and spoke clusters with descriptive internal anchor text (Backlinko). **Moderate.**

## 9. Cannibalization

- **Patrick Stox (Ahrefs):** of 80 sampled multi-ranking keywords, only 1 needed fixing. Act only when two pages share the same intent and one measurably underperforms. **Moderate.**

## 10. Expert disagreements

| Topic | One side | Other side | Working rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keyword density | Roof matches term frequency per zone to top pages | Surfer: irrelevant; Rankability: no correlation | No target percentage; use SERP-relative checks only to catch under- or over-use |
| Exact match in title and H1 | Roof tests: top zones; Dean: front-load | Backlinko: near-zero correlation within page one | Include it; it gets you onto page one, other factors set position |
| Keyword in URL | Roof: a top zone | Surfer: almost no correlation | Include it; zero cost |
| Word count | Longer earns links up to about 1,000 words | No rank correlation; traffic falls past 2,000 | Match the SERP range for the intent |
| "SEO is math" vs semantic authority | Roof's lorem ipsum ranking | GÜBÜR, Surfer: coverage and topical maps | Placement isolates; in competitive niches coverage and authority decide |
| Ranking vs AI citation | Ahrefs July 2025: 76% of cited pages in top 10 | Ahrefs March 2026, new parser: 38% | Ranking helps, not a guarantee; cover fan-out sub-questions |

## 11. Caveats

- Ahrefs, Surfer, Backlinko (owned by Semrush) and Rankability sell tools related to their findings.
- Correlation is not causation; only Roof's tests isolate variables, and they use artificial conditions.
- Several figures are dated (meta descriptions 2020, snippet length 2017 to 2020, Backlinko ranking data about 2019 to 2020). Treat them as benchmarks.
- AI Overview behaviour changes fast; re-check every few months.
