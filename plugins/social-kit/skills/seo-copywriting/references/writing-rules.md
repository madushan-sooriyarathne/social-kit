# Writing Rules

## Placement (do these, then stop counting)

| Zone | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| URL slug | Short, lowercase, hyphenated, contains the primary keyword without stop words: `/villa-hikkaduwa` not `/page?id=12` | Roof, Backlinko |
| Title tag | Primary keyword, front-loaded where natural; about 50 to 60 characters; brand name at the end if it fits; parentheses over brackets | Dean, Zyppy |
| H1 | One per page; contains the primary keyword; close in meaning to the title | Roof, Backlinko, Zyppy |
| Opening | Primary keyword within the first 100 to 150 words, inside a sentence that says what the page offers or answers | Backlinko, Linehan |
| H2 and H3 | Variations, synonyms, secondaries and real questions; never the exact primary in every heading | Surfer |
| Meta description | Benefit plus conversion action, about 120 to 155 characters, includes the keyword naturally | Ahrefs (rewritten often; write for the click) |
| Image alt text | Describe the image accurately; include a keyword only where it is genuinely what the image shows | Accessibility basic, no ranking evidence |
| Internal links | Descriptive anchors pointing to the cluster hub and to the money page | Backlinko |

Exact match or a natural close variant both count. "Luxury villa in Hikkaduwa" covers "luxury villa Hikkaduwa"; do not write broken grammar to force an exact phrase.

## Coverage and information gain

1. Build the coverage checklist from the subtopics and questions that most of the top results share. Cover each one at a depth that matches its importance to the searcher.
2. Add what competitors lack, using the brand's own facts: exact prices or price ranges, inclusions, capacity, distances in minutes, materials, process steps, timelines, guarantees, team credentials, first-party data, honest limitations.
3. Prefer specifics over adjectives. "Eight minutes on foot to Narigama beach" beats "close to the beach".
4. Stay on intent. Tangential sections dilute the page (Ahrefs case study). Put them on their own page and link.
5. Never invent facts. Use `[VERIFY: ...]` for anything not supplied.

## Answer formatting for snippets and AI answers

- Under each question H2, give a direct answer of about 40 to 50 words first, then expand.
- Use numbered lists for processes, bulleted lists for options, and a table for comparisons or rates when the SERP shows those formats.
- Answer the fan-out sub-questions around the topic (cost, duration, best time, who it suits, alternatives), not only the head term. Ahrefs found ranking for these correlated 0.77 with AI Overview citation.
- Keep entity facts consistent with the brand's other profiles (name, location, category, contact), since AI systems cross-check sources.

## Conversion copy that still ranks

- Lead with the outcome the buyer wants, then proof, then the action.
- Introductions: Backlinko's APP (Agree with the problem, Promise the outcome, Preview what follows).
- Page flow: AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action).
- Headlines: the 4 U's (Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific).
- Use customer language from reviews and enquiries; it doubles as long-tail coverage.
- Commercial pages still need substance (what is included, how it works, who it suits, price, FAQs), but not essay-length preamble before the offer.
- Trust signals that are real: reviews with source, named people, policies, guarantees. Indig's user study shows brand trust is the first filter.

## Length

Match the range of pages that rank for the same intent. Stop when the coverage checklist and the conversion path are complete. Pages under 1,000 words win most AI Overview citations; length has no rank correlation on its own.

## Voice and style

- Write in the brand's voice and the market's spelling variant.
- Plain, concrete sentences. Cut filler ("in today's world", "look no further", "nestled", "unparalleled").
- Respect the user's style preferences (for example, no em dashes; restructure the sentence instead).

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a keyword by volume alone.
- Writing a guide when the SERP wants a product, service or listing page.
- Chasing a density figure or repeating the exact keyword in every heading.
- Titles over 60 characters or very short ones.
- Two pages in the set targeting the same intent.
- Padding for length or adding off-intent sections.
- Generic superlatives with no facts behind them.
- Self-promotional "best X" lists ranking the brand first, glossary farms, one-question FAQ pages, mass "X vs Y" pages.
