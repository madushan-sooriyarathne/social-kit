# Research Brief: organic-growth-strategy

## Original request (verbatim)

> research how to successfully manage meta social platforms (content, posts, stories, etc...) for highest reach and growth - only pay attention to material written by 3rd party verified experts. ignore anything written by meta as it is biased.

Original research chat: https://claude.ai/chat/9d67964c-6caf-48fc-b948-253ca0e7f4fb (2026-11-07)

## Excluded

All Meta-authored or Meta-sponsored material, including Instagram and Threads official posts and statements by Meta staff. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## Scope

Organic reach and follower growth on Instagram, Facebook and Threads for brand and business accounts. Not paid ads.

## Questions to keep answered

1. How distribution currently works according to independent analysis.
2. Format performance: Reels, carousels, single images, Stories, Lives, and how it varies by follower tier.
3. Reels specifics: length, hooks, originality rules, editing tools.
4. Posting frequency and timing.
5. Captions, keywords vs hashtags.
6. Collab posts, community management and reply effects.
7. Facebook Pages and Groups, Threads growth.
8. Industry benchmarks and KPIs (engagement, reach rate, follower growth).
9. What to avoid, and which areas still have thin evidence.

## Fast-moving areas (check first every month)

New benchmark releases (Socialinsider, Buffer, Metricool, Dash Social, Quid), recommendation and originality policy changes reported by independent outlets, Threads reach data, Reels length and format changes.

## Watchlist

Socialinsider, Buffer, Metricool, Dash Social, Emplifi, Sprout Social, Quid (Rival IQ), Hootsuite experiments, Virev, eMarketer, Journal of Digital and Social Media Marketing.
