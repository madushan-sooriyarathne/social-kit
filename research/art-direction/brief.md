# Research Brief: art-direction

## Original request (verbatim)

> research how to do art directing for brand shoots (photography) and videography to produce high engagement content for brands in any sector. This includes the angle of the shoot. subject placement and everything else that creates a perfect shot - only pay attention to material written by 3rd party verified experts.

Original research chat: https://claude.ai/chat/4d3bea0c-eaab-4971-ba73-e70d33b1ddaa (2026-11-08)

## Excluded

Platform-authored creative guidance (Meta, TikTok, YouTube or Google, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat). Platform co-produced studies only if flagged. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## Scope

Art direction for brand photography and video that drives engagement and business results in any sector, from pre-production through on-set direction to edit and delivery.

## Questions to keep answered

1. Which image qualities move demand and engagement (information-carrying vs decorative, quality, taste fit).
2. People, faces, hands and gaze.
3. Camera angle, shot sizes, composition and subject placement.
4. Lenses, light, colour and grade.
5. Video: hooks, brand presence timing, story structure, camera movement, pacing, sound and captions.
6. Aspect ratios and multi-format shooting.
7. Attention and effectiveness research on creative.
8. Sector conventions: hospitality, real estate, SaaS, food, fashion, professional services.
9. Myths, weak claims and expert disagreements.

## Fast-moving areas (check first every month)

New attention research, creative effectiveness studies, vertical and short-form video findings, research on AI-generated or AI-edited imagery and its effect on trust.

## Watchlist

Karen Nelson-Field (Amplified Intelligence), System1 and Orlando Wood, IPA, Les Binet and Peter Field, Nielsen Norman Group, Journal of Marketing Research and Marketing Science, Mike Kelley, Bruce Block, Fstoppers education, American Society of Cinematographers.
