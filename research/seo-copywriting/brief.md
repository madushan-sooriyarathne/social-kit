# Research Brief: seo-copywriting

## Original request (verbatim)

> research how to write keyword optimized content COPY to optimize SEO with a proper keyword research - only pay attention to material written by 3rd party verified experts. ignore anything written by google as it is biased.

Original research chat: https://claude.ai/chat/43a76a0b-a1f1-456e-afd8-9006c7fd3643 (2026-11-08)

## Excluded

All Google-authored material, statements by Google employees and Keyword Planner guidance. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## Scope

An end-to-end method from keyword research to publish-ready, keyword-optimized page copy for hospitality, real estate, SaaS and general sites.

## Questions to keep answered

1. Keyword research: seeds, expansion, reading difficulty and volume data, intent from the live SERP, clustering, prioritization.
2. Keyword placement: titles, H1 and H2s, intro, URL, meta, alt text; what controlled tests show.
3. Coverage, originality, repetition and keyword density.
4. Length.
5. Titles and meta descriptions, including rewrite rates.
6. Featured snippets and AI answers: what gets cited.
7. Topical authority, clustering and cannibalization.
8. Copy that converts while still ranking.
9. Where experts disagree.

## Fast-moving areas (check first every month)

AI Overview and AI Mode citation behaviour, title rewrite studies, keyword tool accuracy studies, AI-generated content risk.
