# Research Brief: seo-expert

## Original request (verbatim)

> research how to optimize SEO for successful organic growth- only pay attention to material written by 3rd party verified experts. ignore anything written by google as it is biased.

Original research chat: https://claude.ai/chat/43f05067-92d6-497b-9c88-59c2706f0bd7 (2026-11-08)

## Excluded

All Google-authored material and statements by Google employees. Third-party analysis of the 2024 leak and the US v. Google trial is allowed. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## Scope

What actually drives sustainable organic growth, for hospitality, real estate, local services, SaaS and general sites, including AI search visibility.

## Questions to keep answered

1. What predicts organic winners and losers (independent multi-site studies).
2. Ranking factors with evidence strength.
3. Core and spam updates since the last refresh: who won, who lost, and why according to independent analysts.
4. Content strategy findings: topical focus, information gain, first-party assets.
5. Technical findings: rendering, crawlability, structured data, Core Web Vitals.
6. Links, digital PR and brand mentions.
7. AI search and zero-click: AI Overviews, AI Mode and LLM citations, click-through impact.
8. Measurement, myths and points of expert disagreement.

## Fast-moving areas (check first every month)

Core updates, AI Overviews and AI Mode behaviour, AI citation studies, zero-click data, local search ranking factors.
