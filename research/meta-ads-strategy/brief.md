# Research Brief: meta-ads-strategy

## Original request (verbatim)

> research how to successfully run meta ads - only pay attention to material written by 3rd party verified experts. ignore anything written by meta as it is biased.

Original research chat: https://claude.ai/chat/c2dda979-cd27-4144-b7e2-ac4005735c8e (2026-11-07)

## Excluded

All Meta-authored or Meta-sponsored material. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## Scope

How to run Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads successfully today, for brands in luxury hospitality, real estate, SaaS, ecommerce, local services and small markets including Sri Lanka.

## Questions to keep answered

1. Account and campaign structure: how many campaigns and ad sets, consolidation vs segmentation, Advantage+ vs manual.
2. Creative strategy: testing frameworks, volume, formats, hooks, fatigue, what current creative benchmarks show.
3. Targeting: broad vs interest vs lookalike, how creative now acts as targeting.
4. Tracking and measurement: pixel and Conversions API, attribution settings and changes, incrementality results.
5. Budgeting, bidding and scaling: learning-phase thresholds, cost caps, scaling rules, small-budget approaches.
6. Optimization events and funnel design.
7. Special Ad Category rules (Housing, Financial, Employment) and how they affect real estate.
8. Where the named experts disagree, and whether any have changed position.

## Fast-moving areas (check first every month)

Attribution and reporting changes, Advantage+ behaviour, creative benchmark releases, Special Ad Category rules, learning-phase thresholds, new incrementality data.

## Watchlist

Jon Loomer (jonloomer.com), Andrew Foxwell (foxwelldigital.com), Barry Hott, Charley Tichenor IV, Ben Heath, Common Thread Collective, Motion (creative benchmarks), Haus (incrementality), AdExchanger.
