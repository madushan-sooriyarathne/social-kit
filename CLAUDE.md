# Social Kit repo

A Claude plugin marketplace of research-backed marketing skills.

- When asked to refresh the skills, follow `REFRESH_PROCEDURE.md` exactly, and apply `SOURCE_POLICY.md` to every source.
- Skills live in `plugins/social-kit/skills/`. Research briefs and changelogs live in `research/`.
- Never write em dashes in any file.
- Never merge to `main` yourself during a refresh; open a pull request.
